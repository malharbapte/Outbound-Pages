Home flow: two options

Two ways to answer points 5 and 6: should Range and Stock be one section or two, and where should Stories sit. The mock shows the changed stretch of the homepage at full width, with real specs and drive-away prices from the two live sites. Photos and wording are placeholders; the photos repeat because the stock photos are still to come.

Why: the visitor chooses a model and sees what is on the floor in the same place, so nothing is shown twice. Stories follow straight away, while the shopper is still weighing a van. The cost: one busier section, and a model with nothing in stock has nothing to show.

Why: the two sections stop repeating each other. The Range answers "which model is for me", using Wonderland's own headings (Electrical, Suspension, Water). Stories sit between it and the stock, so proof arrives right before the price. Drive away answers "what can I buy this week", with price first. More scrolling than A, but each part has one clear question.
