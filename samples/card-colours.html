Van card colours: five options

Same card, same layout, same three vans. Only the colour changes, using Tyre, Bone, Olive, Deep Olive, Warm Grey, Rose, Rose Deep, Blue and Orange, and straight mixes of two of them. The card sits on the stock section's Bone background, as it does live; "Today" is the card as it is now, for comparison.

  • Hierarchy through colour. The price is always the brightest thing on the card, then the figures, then the icon lines. Colour is spent on the few things that matter (price, icons, labels) and nowhere else.
  • Harmony. Each option uses one panel colour, one accent and one tint for the numbers, so the two halves of the panel read as one family. The orange tag stays on every card, since it is the site's tag colour.
  • Legibility first. Under each title is the measured contrast for the text, the small labels, the icons and the figures. The usual line is 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for icons and large figures.

Photos and specs are the real listings for the Amaroo 2107, Hornet 1806 and XTR.
