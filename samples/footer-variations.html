Footer: six variations

The footer's content is unchanged (manufacturer, contact, address, hours, legal). Three variations use the diamond, three do not. The Wonderland logo here is also the fixed one: it was being squeezed sideways by the page's image rule and now keeps its own shape.

With the diamond
D1

Waypoints

A road of five diamonds runs across the top, like the Ownership journey's steps: four outlined, the middle one filled. Each label gets a small diamond bullet, the same one the FAQ uses. Smallest change, most consistent with the site.

D2

Lattice

A field of outlined diamonds fades in from the right edge, with a few filled olive ones. Texture without touching the content, which stays on the left and centre.

D3

Sign-off

A big headline closes the page between two diamonds, with a large cropped diamond outline behind it. The loudest of the three, and the only one that adds new words, which are placeholder wording.

Without the diamond
N1

Wordmark

A huge outlined OUTBOUND, cropped by the bottom of the page. Pure type, no new shapes: the brand name becomes the texture.

N2

Call to action first

An olive band leads, with a question, the phone number and a button to the stock; the usual black columns sit beneath. The most commercial of the six, and it puts the page's goal at the very end.

N3

Ridgeline

A quiet mountain silhouette along the top edge and a dashed road above the columns, echoing the logo's peaks and the open-road idea. Illustration instead of geometry.
