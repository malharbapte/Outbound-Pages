Suspension icon: four options

What I looked at first: stock icon libraries and design-system guides for how suspension is normally drawn, and what makes a small icon read. The notes:

  • How suspension is usually drawn. Three metaphors recur: the shock absorber (a damper with a ring at each end), a coil spring seen side-on, and a wheel under a spring. A spring on its own is the weak one. Drawn as a zigzag it reads as barbed wire or a lightning bolt, which is exactly what the current icon does.
  • Make the spring read as a spring. The reliable trick is to draw it as a few parallel slanted strokes between two flat seats, with a rod through the middle, rather than a pointed zigzag. The flat seats and mounting rings are what say "mechanical part".
  • Icon rules from the design systems (Atlassian, Sky, Morningstar, Singapore Gov). Use the least detail that carries the metaphor, keep one stroke weight across the set, and match the set before you match the concept. So all four here are 24 grid, 1.5 stroke, round caps, and drawn to sit beside the droplet and bolt.
  • Wonderland's vans mostly run airbag suspension (Cruisemaster and Tuff-Ride, with coil on the Amaroo), so one option shows an airbag instead of a spring.
